# Art and type

- `assets/icons/*.svg` and `assets/icon.svg`: original project vector illustrations. Regenerate card illustrations with `tools/generate_art.ps1`.
- `assets/art/office_garden.png`: original background generated with the built-in image generation tool for this project. It is bundled locally; the game makes no image-generation or network request.
- `assets/fonts/PatrickHand-Regular.ttf`: Patrick Hand from Google Fonts, under the bundled `PatrickHand-OFL.txt`.
- `assets/fonts/Nunito.ttf`: Nunito from Google Fonts, under the bundled `Nunito-OFL.txt`.
- `assets/audio/phase_1.wav`, `phase_2.wav`, `phase_3.wav`: original 16-second ambient loops rendered offline by `tools/generate_audio.gd`; runtime plays local files and changes the loop with the company phase.
- Art direction references: [Stacklands](https://sokpop.itch.io/stacklands) and [Clickyland](https://sokpop.itch.io/clickyland) by Sokpop. Their assets are not copied into this project.

## Background prompt

Original production-ready landscape 16:9 illustration for a cozy 2D card-stacking business game. Bird's-eye storybook miniature office-park garden, pale sage and mint grass, cream paving, dark desaturated green imperfect ink outlines, rounded playful shapes and sparse paper texture. A garage upper left, office upper right, hedges, trees, benches, bicycles, flowerpots, a path and delivery van restricted to the perimeter. Empty calm center for a freely movable card table. Soft shadows from upper left. No letters, text, logos, cards, UI, frames, or table. Flat ink and gouache, no neon, 3D or photographic elements.
